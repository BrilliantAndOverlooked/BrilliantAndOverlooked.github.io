Standards & Disclosures

Privacy & AI Transparency

1. Zero Tracking on This Website

This site is a static archive hosted on GitHub Pages. We do not use advertising pixels, third-party analytics cookies, or user accounts. Embedded YouTube videos use privacy-enhanced mode (youtube-nocookie.com) and only load after you explicitly press Play.

2. How AI Tools Are Used

Both the video and audio across Brilliant & Overlooked are produced using AI tools alongside handcrafted 3D animation (Cinema 4D), custom procedural scientific diagrams, and real acoustic orchestral sample libraries. All videos carry YouTube’s altered/synthetic media disclosure. Whenever a historical figure’s verbatim documented words are quoted, they are read by an AI voice and clearly labelled on screen.

3. Archival Portraits & Source Integrity

Every historical photograph on our channel and website is sourced from verified public-domain archives or Creative Commons licences with full attribution. Real photographs are kept strictly rigid — we never deepfake, warp, or AI-animate real people’s faces. Every factual claim is verified across two independent scholarly or archival sources.
